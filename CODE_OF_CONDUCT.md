# Code of conduct

PlayFetch contributors are expected to make participation respectful, constructive, and harassment-free for everyone.

Examples of acceptable behavior include showing empathy, giving actionable technical feedback, respecting differing viewpoints, and focusing on what is best for users and the project. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, doxxing, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions or interactions that violate these expectations and may temporarily or permanently ban repeat offenders. Report conduct concerns privately to the project owner through their GitHub profile. All reports will be reviewed fairly and kept as confidential as practical.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
